Notes on My Creative Process

Design
Reflection

✦   ✧   ✦

So far, I have noticed that I enjoy creating websites with strong visual styles and interesting details. I like the feeling of a webpage that uses clean typography, dark backgrounds, and small decorative elements to create a specific mood. I also like using animation to make a page feel more alive.

My projects have different visual styles, but they share a focus on visual storytelling and overall atmosphere. They also differ in their layouts, colors, and use of movement. Some designs are simple and organized, while others use more experimental compositions and animations.

Through these projects, I have learned how CSS can change the appearance and behavior of a website. I have also learned that small details, such as spacing, typography, and responsive layouts, can make a big difference.

Next, I want to experiment with more creative animations, unusual layouts, and interactions that respond to screen size. I also want to improve my ability to turn visual ideas into working websites.
